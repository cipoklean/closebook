The problem

Cross-border money is messy.

Payments arrive without references

Customers pay 4,400 when the invoice says 4,500, under a slightly different name.

Month-end takes days

Someone matches bank lines to invoices by hand, one by one.

One wrong click pays a supplier twice

Payouts that fail, then succeed, are easy to send again by mistake.

How it works

From money in to suppliers paid, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Money comes in

    Airwallex tells Closebook the moment a payment lands.

  2. 2

    The AI suggests a match

    It reads the payer, the amount and the reference, then picks the likely invoice.

  3. 3

    Your rules decide

    Confidence, amounts, limits and the safety buffer are checked by code, not the AI.

  4. 4

    Suppliers get paid

    Currency is bought and payments sent through Airwallex. Big ones wait for your OK.

Safety

The AI suggests. Your rules decide.

The AI is good at guessing who paid what. It is never the one that moves money. Every suggestion goes through rules you set, written in plain code. When they're not satisfied, Closebook waits and asks you.
